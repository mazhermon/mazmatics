# Mazmatics — Design Context

> Persistent design guidelines for the impeccable skill set. Sourced from `PRODUCT.md`, `DESIGN.md`, the redesign-may brainstorm, and Maz's standing preferences. Update in place when direction shifts; do **not** re-run teach-impeccable to overwrite this file.

## Design Context

### Users

**Primary:** parents (and gift-buying caregivers) of kids aged 7–10 who are math-reluctant, math-anxious, or losing interest in maths early in school. Often a parent who themselves had a complicated relationship with math and is looking for a *non-screen* option that doesn't feel like more homework. Mostly arriving on **mobile**, frequently from a word-of-mouth referral or social link, in a 30-second skim-and-judge moment. The single biggest moment-of-arrival job: "is this legit and is this for my kid?"

**Secondary:** grandparents buying gifts; teachers evaluating supplementary classroom resources. NZ/AU is the largest visitor segment (Maz is NZ-based and most word-of-mouth circulates in NZ + Australia).

**The kid the book is for** is also implicitly an audience signal even though they don't buy: ages 7–10, US grade 2–4, likes drawing, codes, stories, silly humour ("Who doesn't love a good poop emoji?!" — actual brand copy). The site should reflect respect for kids' intelligence — never infantilise.

### Brand Personality

**Three words:** warm, scrappy, growth-mindset.

- **Warm** — speaks like a parent who's been there, not an educator with a clipboard. First-person plural ("we"), second-person ("you"). The author's voice (Maz Hermon) is present and visible — this is not a faceless edu brand.
- **Scrappy** — hand-drawn / kid-coded / arcade. Rough edges are a feature. The book itself is black-and-white pages, "not precious — draw on it, rip a page out and stick it on your wall."
- **Growth-mindset** — confident in its choices ("we haven't included an answers page"), pushes back gently on the cultural assumption that maths is something kids should just survive.

**Locale-aware word swap:** the brand line "I like math" stays fixed (US spelling). Every other "math/maths" usage routes through `AppContext.mathsWord`. Never hardcode either form.

### Aesthetic Direction

**Hybrid: editorial backbone, scrappy/hand-drawn soul, retro-arcade accents.** Strict role hierarchy — these three flavours don't compete on the same surface.

- **Editorial backbone (B):** Outfit body, generous whitespace, clean type hierarchy. Carries readability and SEO content.
- **Scrappy soul (A):** real hand-drawn artwork from the book (`Mazmatics-a-plus-001..006.jpg`), the `ILikeMaths` SVG, character illustrations (Lindy the Giant Girl, Pac-Man-style "Circle Betty", sun sprite). This is where warmth comes from.
- **Retro-arcade accents (C):** Bungee Shade display face for short hero/footer wordmarks ("MAZMATICS", "FUN MATH 4 KIDS BOOK", "GET THE BOOK"). Pacifico cursive used in **exactly one place**: the hero "I like math" wordmark. Yellow `Banner` with animated waves for the "Will you help your kids with maths today?" attention-grab below the hero.

**Colors (locked):**
- Primary: `--purple-2` (#ba90ff), `--purple-3` (#8c5fd5)
- Accent blues: `--blue-1` (#dff9fc), `--blue-4` (#47a5f1), `--blue-5` (#207bc5)
- Yellow accent: `--yellow-1` (#fff73e) — used for the persistent CTA pill, the Banner, attention moments
- Greens: `--green-1`, `--green-2` — secondary accents
- The brand gradient `--gradient-1` (purple → blue, left-to-right) is a brand asset, not a generic AI tell. Use it intentionally on the H2 wordmark, the buy CTA pill, and the `bigStripeDeco` band — not as wallpaper.
- **Gradient text contrast:** `--blue-4` is the gradient's lightest stop and fails AA on white when applied to letterforms. Pre-existing on the site; document but don't reach for darker stops without Maz's approval since it changes the brand feel.

**Type:**
- `--font-headings-display`: Bungee Shade (loaded via Google Fonts). Single source of truth — never hardcode 'Bungee Shade' in a component. Maz may swap this post-redesign, so any new code should use the variable.
- `--font-script`: Pacifico (loaded locally via @font-face). Reserved for the home hero "I like math" wordmark only — do not expand without explicit approval.
- `--font-headings`, `--font-copy`: Outfit. Body, H2, H3, button text.
- `--font-headings-fun`: Daruma. Currently unused; keep the slot.

**Anti-references — what Mazmatics is NOT:**
- Not Khan Academy / IXL / Mathnasium — not drill-and-kill, not screen-based, not score-driven.
- Not a textbook — no answer keys, no completion badges.
- Not over-polished corporate edu (Komodo, Splash Math) — those feel sterile.
- Not infantilising — respects kids' intelligence.
- Not generic-AI aesthetic — no glassmorphism, no cyan-on-dark, no hero metric layout, no identical card grids, no glowing dark mode. The purple→blue gradient is an existing brand element that predates AI gradient trends; it is not a tell **in this context** but should not be expanded into wallpaper.
- **No external visual references.** Maz directed: "amplify what's already there." Do not reach for outside aesthetics — mature what exists.

### Design Principles

1. **Conversion-first.** Every page ≤2 clicks to an Amazon buy. Trust signals (social proof, author warmth, real photos of kids using the book) belong above the fold or near it.
2. **Mobile is primary.** Most parent traffic comes from social → mobile. Optimise for skim in the first 30 seconds.
3. **Author proximity is the differentiator.** Maz's photo, voice, and the book's origin (built with his kids) is what cold-corporate competitors can't fake. Surface it; don't bury it on /about.
4. **Amplify existing brand.** The site already has the right wheelhouse — the work is maturing it, not redirecting. Reuse existing components, characters, and assets before reaching for new ones. **Make changes section by section** — don't sweep through visual refactors that ripple unpredictably.
5. **Accessibility floor: WCAG 2.2 AA, AAA where pragmatic.** Every component must pass keyboard, focus, contrast, alt-text, landmarks, reduced-motion checks. Document deliberate AA-not-AAA tradeoffs in the spec or commit message rather than failing silently.

## Behavioural rules for assistants

- **Never hardcode "math" or "maths"** — route through `AppContext.mathsWord`.
- **Never hardcode `'Bungee Shade'`** — use `var(--font-headings-display)`.
- **Pacifico is reserved** for the home hero "I like math" wordmark only.
- **NZ default:** when locale/timezone is unknown, default to AU storefront (covers NZ visitors who ship from Amazon Australia). See `lib/locale.ts:resolveCountry`.
- **Section-by-section changes only.** A previous attempt at a full visual rebuild lost the brand voice. The full marketing-rebuild branch is preserved at `redesign-may-marketing-spike` for reference; copy improvements from it are documented in `docs/redesign-may/MARKETING-COPY-CHANGES.md` for staged re-application.
- **Confirm before any sweeping visual change.** Token shifts, divider system rewrites, page restructures — all require explicit approval before merging.
