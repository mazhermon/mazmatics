# Reactivate Instagram + Facebook — the "Mazmatics has a new home" pulse

You have two dormant social accounts and a freshly redesigned site that nobody knows about. This step is the announcement — one post per platform, plus bio updates, so anyone who already follows you sees the relaunch in their feed and the new visitors who land on your profile see a current bio + link.

**Time:** 20 minutes total.

**You'll need:** your phone or laptop logged into Instagram + Facebook. A single image (book cover or a kid's-desk shot or a hero crop from the new site). The post copy below.

---

## Step 1 — pick a post copy variant (5 min)

Pick **one** of these. Don't agonize. The brand voice is warm + scrappy + parent-friendly; any of these fits.

### Variant A — straight relaunch (shortest, most direct)

> Mazmatics has a new home. Same scrappy maths joy, fresh look.
>
> Take a peek → mazmatics.com

### Variant B — story-led (gentle, personal)

> A new front door for the same book.
>
> Mazmatics has a redesigned site — built so parents can find what's in the book faster, see the actual pages before buying, and (if it's clicked for your kid) leave a review without hunting for the link.
>
> mazmatics.com

### Variant C — warm + future-oriented

> Mazmatics has a new home. The book is the same — kids 7–10, drawing, decoding, the occasional silly maths joke. The site is fresh, faster, and ready for what's next.
>
> Vol. 2 is in the works. The mailing list is the first place we'll say it's out.
>
> mazmatics.com

### Variant D — ask-led (drives email signup directly)

> The redesigned Mazmatics site is up at mazmatics.com.
>
> If you've got the book already — there's a fresh place to leave a quick review (the biggest help we can ask for). And if you'd like a note when Vol. 2 ships, the mailing list is now live on the site.
>
> Have a peek →

**My pick if you can't decide:** Variant C. Mentions Vol. 2 (builds anticipation), points at the mailing list (the highest-leverage call), keeps the tone warm.

## Step 2 — pick an image (5 min)

One image per post. **Square (1080×1080) for Instagram works on Facebook too.** Don't make two images — one image, posted twice.

Ranked by signal-to-effort:

1. **A kid's hand drawing in the book** — high-signal, hard to fake authenticity, social-shareable. If you have a recent photo of one of your kids using the book, that's gold. (Get parental permission if it's anyone else's kid.)
2. **The book on a desk with crayons / pencils / coffee** — easy to stage, looks "real" rather than "marketing," ties to the "home play not homework" voice.
3. **Hero crop from the new site** — screenshot of Lindy with the headline, cropped to square. Shows the redesign directly, but feels more "look at our website" than "look at the book."
4. **The book cover alone on a plain background** — last resort. Cleanest but most product-shotty. Use only if no other option.

If you have 60 seconds, take a fresh phone shot of the book on your kitchen table right now. That beats anything else.

---

## Step 3 — post on Instagram (5 min)

1. Open Instagram, tap **+** (create), choose **Post**.
2. Upload your image. Crop to square if it isn't already.
3. **Caption:** paste your chosen variant.
4. **First-comment trick:** Instagram doesn't make external URLs in captions clickable, so add a separator and the URL in plain text at the bottom of the caption. Visitors will still type it / copy-paste — small friction but unavoidable. Or, even better, **update your bio link first (Step 5)** and refer to it in the caption: *"Link in bio →"*.
5. **Hashtags** — keep it modest. Too many feels spammy on the algorithm. **Pick 4–6 from this list:**
   - `#mazmatics`
   - `#funmathforkids`
   - `#funmaths` (UK/AU/NZ audience)
   - `#mathforkids` / `#mathsforkids`
   - `#homeschoolmath` / `#homeschoolmaths`
   - `#kidsbooks`
   - `#kidsactivitybooks`
   - `#wellington` (local discoverability)
   - `#nzauthors` / `#aussieauthors` (if relevant)
   - `#parentingnz`
6. **Best time to post on IG (NZ time):**
   - Tuesday or Thursday, 7–9pm NZT (parents on phone after kids in bed)
   - Avoid weekends — engagement is lower for educational content
7. **Tap "Share."**
8. **Critical:** after posting, **reply to every comment in the first 24 hours**, even just a heart or "thanks!". Instagram's algorithm reads early-comment-reply patterns as engagement signal and pushes the post wider. This is the single most important thing you can do in the first hour after posting.

---

## Step 4 — mirror to Facebook (3 min)

Easiest path: cross-post from Instagram automatically (if you've linked the accounts). If not:

1. Open Facebook → your `mazmaticsfunforkids` page → **Create post**.
2. Upload the same image, paste the same caption.
3. Facebook DOES make URLs in captions clickable — **paste the full `https://mazmatics.com` in the caption.** Facebook will auto-fetch the OG preview (which we just verified) so the link appears as a card under the post.
4. Hashtags — Facebook largely ignores them in algorithmic ranking, but include 2–3 for searchability (`#mazmatics`, `#funmathforkids`, plus one geographic).
5. Post.
6. Pin the post to the top of the page (page settings → "Pin to top of page") so anyone visiting the page sees it first for the next month.

## Step 5 — update both bios (2 min)

The bio is what every new visitor sees first. After a long dormant period, the bio probably says something out of date.

### Instagram bio (max ~150 chars)

```
Fun Math 4 Kids — activity + story book for kids 7–10.
By Maz Hermon, NZ. New site + Vol. 2 coming.
mazmatics.com
```

That's 130 chars — fits easily. Add an emoji or two if it feels too plain (📚 ✏️ work; skip if you're not into them).

### Facebook page "About" section

Slightly more room here:

```
Mazmatics: Fun Math 4 Kids Vol. 1 — an activity and story book for kids 7–10 (US grade 2–4). Drawing, decoding, silly maths jokes, home play not homework. By Maz Hermon, a Wellington dad. Vol. 2 in the works.

mazmatics.com
```

While you're in the bio editor, also confirm:
- Profile picture is the Mazmatics logo (not Maz's personal photo).
- Page category is **"Book"** or **"Book series"** (not "Personal blog" or similar).
- The website field has `https://mazmatics.com`.

## What to expect in the first 24 hours

- **Engagement will be modest.** A dormant account doesn't bounce back in one post — the algorithm has to relearn that the account exists. Don't measure success by likes on this first post; measure by whether you sustain a 2-post-per-week rhythm for the next month (per the marketing playbook).
- **Some old followers will reply with "oh nice, didn't realise you'd updated!" or "wait — Vol. 2?"** — engage with every one. These are your warmest possible advocates.
- **Profile visits will spike** — people see the post in their feed, click through to your profile to remember who you are. The bio is doing real work here.
- **Mailing list signups will likely tick up over the next week.** Both platforms will quietly drive traffic to the site from the bio link. Check MailerLite in 7 days.

---

## What NOT to do

- **Don't post to both platforms simultaneously and wait for "viral" results.** Treat this as a single deliberate gentle pulse, not a campaign launch.
- **Don't apologize for the dormant period** in the caption. Nobody noticed. Don't draw attention to it.
- **Don't tag big accounts** (e.g. `@nzmaths`, `@parents`). It reads as desperate. They'll find you naturally if your content is good.
- **Don't run paid ads off this post.** With low base engagement, paid amplification burns money on visitors who bounce. Reviews + content first; paid only after the flywheel exists.
